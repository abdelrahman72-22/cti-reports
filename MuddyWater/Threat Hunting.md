# Threat Hunting – APT29

## Hunt Hypotheses

- Systems that received phishing emails subsequently exhibiting script execution or persistence changes.
    
- Hosts with repeated outbound HTTPS connections inconsistent with their baseline role or function.
    

## Notes

MuddyWater activity tends to repeat recognizable behavioral patterns rather than introduce novel techniques. Successful hunting is more likely to focus on campaign linkage and persistence auditing than on identifying unique malware signatures.
