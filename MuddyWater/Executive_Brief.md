# Executive Brief – APT29

MuddyWater is an Iran-aligned espionage actor focused on maintaining long-term access to government and infrastructure-related networks rather than achieving rapid or disruptive outcomes.

The primary risk to the organization lies in prolonged, low-visibility compromise that enables intelligence collection over extended periods. Such access may expose sensitive communications, operational details, and strategic information without triggering immediate detection.

Business impact includes potential loss of confidential information, regulatory exposure, and degradation of strategic trust.

Recommended posture emphasizes phishing resilience, behavioral monitoring, and sustained detection of low-noise persistence rather than reliance on single-event alerts or advanced malware indicators.
