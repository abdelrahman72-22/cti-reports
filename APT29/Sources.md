# Sources – APT29

- **Microsoft Threat Intelligence** – Primary source for recent APT29 activity, identity-centric operations, cloud abuse, and post-SolarWinds tradecraft.
    
- **Mandiant Threat Intelligence (Google Cloud)** – Authoritative reporting on APT29 campaigns, operational behavior, and the SolarWinds (SUNBURST) compromise.
    
- **CrowdStrike Intelligence** – Actor profiling and tradecraft analysis, including clear differentiation between APT29 and other Russian-aligned threat actors.
    
- **MITRE ATT&CK (G0016)** – Reference mapping for consistent techniques and behaviors observed across multiple campaigns.
    
- **CISA / Joint Cybersecurity Advisories** – Government-issued advisories related to Russian SVR cyber activity and defensive guidance.

## Notes

- Reporting on APT29 shows **high consistency across primary vendors**, particularly regarding long-term access, stealth, and identity-focused tradecraft.
    
- Public reporting is biased toward **high-visibility incidents** (e.g., SolarWinds), likely underrepresenting quieter, long-dwell operations.
    
- Government advisories prioritize defensive recommendations and may omit operational nuance.
    
- Attribution confidence is high, but full victim scope and internal tasking priorities remain partially opaque.
