# Executive Brief – APT29

APT29 is a state-sponsored espionage actor focused on gaining long-term, covert access to sensitive information rather than causing disruption.

The primary risk to the organization is prolonged, undetected access to cloud-based email and identity systems, which may enable strategic intelligence collection over time.

Business impact includes potential exposure of sensitive communications, regulatory risk, and loss of strategic confidentiality.

Recommended posture includes prioritizing cloud identity security, monitoring authentication behavior, and preparing for long-dwell intrusions rather than short-lived incidents.
